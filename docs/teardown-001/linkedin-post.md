# Draft LinkedIn post

I'm rebuilding flavius.pro around my design engineering service.

The old portfolio was a listening bar: projects on a shelf, experience as a tab, Schoolbell handwriting everywhere. I still like the character of it. But a founder arriving with a live app had to work out what I could do for them.

The new site starts with that app. I redesign AI-built apps and small SaaS products directly in their React codebase, then deliver reviewable pull requests.

A few decisions made the difference to the page:

- Inter handles the offer, prices, scope and forms. Handwriting points at screen decisions.
- Content sits on the page. Pricing needs two readable rows, not a stack of decorated cards.
- The EUR 300 audit is a recorded walkthrough, ten ranked fixes and one screen preview. A polish sprint, from EUR 1,500, is implementation in the codebase. The page makes that boundary explicit.
- The original projects, experience, writing and interests now live on About. Their old links still have somewhere to go.

I'm using my own open design system, flavius-design-taste. Editorial calm carries the commercial pages; Personal room supplies a few annotations.

The comparison assets are still being prepared, so they are labeled placeholders. The Pricele example has a real current screenshot and states what it cannot prove. No invented client names, testimonials or conversion lifts.

The refactor is in a reviewable PR. Checkout and email delivery setup are on the launch TODO list.
