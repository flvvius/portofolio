# Draft X thread

## 1

I'm rebuilding flavius.pro around the work I want to do: redesign AI-built apps and small SaaS products in their React codebase. Design decisions, implemented as reviewable PRs.

## 2

The old site was a listening bar. Projects were objects on a shelf, experience was a tab, and all the text was handwritten. It said a lot about me. It didn't explain what someone could hire me for.

## 3

The new opening starts with the buyer's app: "Your app works. Let's make it feel finished." The next lines name the stack, the deliverable and the EUR 300 audit. The portfolio moves to About.

## 4

I kept the handwriting, but gave it a smaller job. Inter handles prices, scope and UI. Handwritten arrows belong on before/after screens, where they can point at an actual decision.

## 5

Two pricing rows, separated by hairlines. An audit is a walkthrough, ten ranked fixes and one screen preview. A sprint is code in PRs. Those are different purchases, so the page says exactly where one ends and the other starts.

## 6

The proof is still being prepared. Placeholders say PLACEHOLDER. Pricele has a real current capture, but I won't turn that into a fictional before/after. There are no invented quotes, clients or conversion numbers.

## 7

I used my own open design system for this: Editorial calm for the page, Personal room for the annotations. The code and review evidence are in the PR. Payment and delivery setup are still on the launch TODO list.
